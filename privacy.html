Wonderlings: Learn with Pandi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Developer: MCSoftLab · App: com.mclabs.wonderlings

In short: Wonderlings is a learning game for children aged 3–6. We do not collect any personal information that identifies your child or you. There are no ads and no advertising ID is used. To improve the game we collect only anonymous usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics); this data is automatically deleted after 2 months, and a parent can turn this collection off at any time. Game progress is stored only on your device.

1. Information we collect

The app does not request or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, photos, voice recordings, contacts or the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID). There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Anonymous usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which parts of the game are hard and how to improve it. Only the following is collected through this service:

  • App interactions: events such as starting or completing a level, time spent in the game, screens opened and in-game progress
  • Device or other IDs: a random app instance ID that Firebase assigns to the installation (reset when the app is uninstalled), plus device model, operating system and app version, device language and approximate country-level region

This information cannot be linked to a person and is used only for analytics. The advertising ID is not collected; Google signals, granular location and device data collection and ads personalization are turned off. The data is not sold and is not used for advertising. Google processes this data on our behalf and under our instructions as a service provider. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and automatically deleted after 2 months.

Turning it off: A parent can turn off usage statistics at any time under Parents → Settings → Privacy. When it is off, the app sends no data to Firebase Analytics.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or any other server:

  • Game progress (completed levels, stars, stickers), Pandi’s status, crystals and items
  • Settings (language, sound, daily time limit, notification preference) and the weekly play-time report

You can delete this data at any time via Parents → Settings → Privacy → Delete all data on this device, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Notifications

If a parent allows it, the app shows local reminders created on the device when Pandi gets hungry or the daily gift is ready (at most 2 a day, never at night). No notifications are sent from a server. They can be turned off in the Parents area or in the device settings.

5. Children’s privacy

The app is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases, external links and settings are protected by a parent gate. The app was built with the principles of the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) in mind.

6. Security

Audio files and anonymous usage statistics are transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Because we collect no personal data that identifies you, there is no data about you or your child on our servers.

7. Your rights

Because we collect no personal data that identifies you, we hold no data that can be identified as yours to delete, correct or export. Anonymous usage statistics cannot be linked to a person, so individual deletion requests cannot be matched; this data is automatically deleted after 2 months. You can turn collection off as described in section 1 and delete game data on your device as described in section 2. Contact us with any questions.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. Any material change to our data practices will also be announced in the app’s Parents area.
